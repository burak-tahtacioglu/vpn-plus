VPN+ Support

Setup help, troubleshooting and purchase assistance

Before you begin: VPN+ is a client application. It does not provide a VPN account, hosted server, bandwidth or configuration. You need a compatible profile from a server or provider you trust.

How do I add a server?

Open Servers and choose Add Server, Scan QR or Paste Link. VPN+ supports compatible manual profiles, links, QR codes, JSON files and HTTPS subscription URLs. Never import a configuration from someone you do not trust.

Why can’t I connect?

Confirm that the server address, port, protocol and credentials are correct. Check that the profile is active, your internet connection works without the tunnel, and the server is reachable. Packet Tunnel connections must be tested on a physical iPhone or iPad.

Why does the VPN permission appear?

iOS displays a system permission before an app can install and start a Packet Tunnel configuration. VPN+ requires this permission to route traffic through the profile you selected.

How do I restore VPN+ Pro?

Open the VPN+ Pro screen and select Restore while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. VPN+ Pro is designed as a one-time, non-consumable purchase—not a VPN service subscription.

Does VPN+ include free servers?

No. Demo profiles shown in development screenshots are fictional and are not included as working production servers. VPN+ does not sell or operate proxy infrastructure.

Where is my data stored?

Profiles and preferences are stored locally. Passwords and private subscription URLs use Apple Keychain. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Request support

Open a support request in the project’s GitHub Issues area and include:

  • VPN+ version and iOS/iPadOS version
  • Device model
  • Protocol name (without credentials)
  • What you expected and what happened
  • Sanitized diagnostics, if relevant

Do not include passwords, private keys, subscription URLs, access tokens, complete QR codes or personally sensitive traffic details.
