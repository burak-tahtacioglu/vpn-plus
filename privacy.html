Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

In short: VPN+ is a bring-your-own-server proxy client. The app does not operate a VPN service and does not include hosted servers. The current app contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how VPN+ Shield (displayed on your device as “VPN+”) handles information when you use the iOS application. It does not govern the independent proxy servers, DNS resolvers, subscription providers or websites you choose to use.

2. Information stored on your device

VPN+ may store the following information locally so its features can operate:

  • Server profile details, routing rules, DNS preferences and application settings.
  • Profile credentials and private subscription URLs, protected using Apple Keychain.
  • Connection duration, upload/download totals and recent traffic history.
  • Sanitized connection lifecycle diagnostics that do not intentionally include passwords, access tokens or browsing content.

Some data is shared locally between the app and its Packet Tunnel extension through the app’s private App Group container. You can remove locally stored data by deleting profiles in the app or uninstalling VPN+.

3. Network traffic and third-party services

When you connect, network traffic is routed according to the server profile, DNS resolver and rules you selected. Your chosen proxy operator, DNS provider, internet provider and destination services may process network information under their own terms and privacy policies. VPN+ does not control those third parties.

When you add or refresh an HTTPS profile subscription, VPN+ contacts the URL you provided to download compatible configuration data. When you run a connectivity check, the app contacts the relevant server or test endpoint required to perform that check.

4. Camera and QR codes

Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a profile QR code. QR recognition occurs through Apple’s camera frameworks. VPN+ does not upload camera images to the developer.

5. Purchases

Purchases and restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The developer does not receive your full payment-card information. Apple may provide transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock purchased features.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. VPN+ does not sell personal information or VPN traffic data.

7. Data retention and security

Locally stored information remains until you remove it, reset the relevant feature or uninstall the app. VPN+ uses iOS security facilities such as Keychain and application sandboxing. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and you are responsible for choosing trustworthy server and subscription providers.

8. Children’s privacy

VPN+ is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date shown above will be revised when material changes are published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the instructions on the VPN+ Support page. Please do not send server passwords, private keys, access tokens, complete configuration links or traffic logs containing sensitive information.
